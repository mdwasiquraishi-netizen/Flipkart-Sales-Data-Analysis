# Flipkart-Sales-Data-Analysis
## Introduction
The Flipkart Sales Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance using Flipkart product and order data.The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales, orders, ratings, average order value, product categories, brands, products, and yearly/monthly performance.

## Objectives
The primary objective of this project is to transform raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights that can help understand sales trends, customer preferences, top-performing products, and category performance.

## Dataset Information
The dataset contains Flipkart e-commerce product/order-related information used to analyze sales and customer activity.

The analysis focuses on attributes such as:

Product Name
Product Category
Brand
Sales/Order Value
Product Rating
Order information
Year
Month
Quarter
Product-level information

## Observations
1. Overall Sales Performance

The dashboard shows approximately 36M in total sales generated across the analyzed period.

The KPI section provides a quick overview of the overall business performance.

2. Order Performance

The dashboard records approximately 16K total orders, providing a useful overview of customer purchasing activity.

The Order by Year visualization allows comparison between 2015 and 2016.

3. Top-Performing Brands

Karatcraft is the highest-performing brand among the brands displayed, generating approximately 5.6M in sales.

Other strong-performing brands include:

Radiant Bay
BlueStone
Durian
ARRA
Rakam
Jewelss
4. Product Performance

The Sales by Product Name chart identifies the highest-selling products.

Several products generate significantly higher sales than the remaining products, indicating that a relatively small number of products contribute substantially to overall revenue.

5. Category Performance

The dashboard provides both Sales by Product Category and Order by Product Category analysis.

Clothing appears to be the strongest category in terms of order volume, followed by categories such as:

Jewellery
Footwear
Automotive
Home Decor
Beauty & Personal Care
Kitchen & Dining
Computers
Watches
Mobile Accessories
6. Monthly Sales Trends

The Sales by Year & Month visualization helps identify changes in sales performance across months.

The dashboard indicates that some months contribute considerably more sales than others, suggesting potential seasonality or promotional effects.

7. Customer Ratings

The dashboard contains approximately 6.83K ratings.

The rating KPI and rating-related analysis can be used to understand customer engagement and product satisfaction.

## Recommendations
1. Focus on Top-Performing Brands

Brands such as Karatcraft, Radiant Bay, and BlueStone should receive additional marketing and promotional attention because of their strong sales contribution.

2. Strengthen High-Order Categories

Since Clothing and Jewellery show strong order volumes, Flipkart could:

Increase product variety
Offer targeted promotions
Improve inventory availability
Introduce category-specific campaigns
3. Promote High-Selling Products

Top-selling products should be given greater visibility through:

Homepage promotions
Recommended products
Discount campaigns
Cross-selling
Personalized recommendations
4. Investigate Low-Performing Categories

Categories with relatively low order volumes should be analyzed further to identify whether the problem is related to:

Pricing
Product availability
Customer demand
Product quality
Marketing
Competition
5. Use Seasonal Sales Trends

Monthly sales patterns can help management plan:

Inventory
Marketing campaigns
Discounts
Promotional events
Stock replenishment
6. Improve Customer Experience

Ratings and customer feedback should be monitored regularly to identify products with poor customer satisfaction and take corrective action.

## Conclusions
The Flipkart Sales Analytics Dashboard demonstrates how Power BI can transform raw e-commerce data into an interactive business intelligence solution.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of ₹36M sales, 16K orders, 6.83K ratings, and ₹2.22K average order value, while allowing users to analyze performance by product, brand, category, year, month, and quarter.
